# Product Blueprint

MarketFit answers four questions in order: is there a confirmed blocker, what must be verified, what evidence matches or is missing, and what action is proportionate. A range is only an evidence-based fit estimate when CV/JD quality and extraction quality are sufficient; it is never a hiring outcome prediction.

The side panel deliberately supports only user-clicked visible-page capture and local text extraction from a user-selected PDF resume. It does not expose manual JD editing, apply, message recruiters, scroll result lists, access hidden APIs, or bypass job-board controls. Candidate identity is not a scoring input: nationality is represented only through user-provided work authorization, sponsorship need, and valid role-specific restrictions.

The local MVP is structured into extraction, profile, analysis, market, privacy, and UI modules. Market claims are dated source records, while employer eligibility, role sponsorship willingness, and candidate route eligibility remain separate unknowns unless direct evidence exists.
